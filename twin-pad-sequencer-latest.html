Hardware controls

CLR (on screen): toggles clear mode, then press a Pad A right-column track button to clear that track, a Pad B right-column lane button to clear that lane of the selected track, a bottom-row note pad to clear that step on the selected track, or the Play button to clear everything. Clear mode is one-shot and clears notes only (velocity, gate, mutes and loops are kept). Press CLR again to cancel.

Launchpad models: choose the model of each pad in the MIDI devices section (default LP Mini MK3). LP X uses the same protocol as the Mini MK3 with its own SysEx ID. LP Pro means the Launchpad Pro / Pro MK2 (SysEx ID 0x10, Programmer layout, 6-bit RGB). LP X-Y Mode and LP Drum Mode are for the original Launchpad: the app selects the layout itself. Both use all eight top-row buttons (CC 68h–6Fh) and 4-level red/green LEDs only (blue colours show as green, the dark blue loop range is barely visible, there is no logo LED). X-Y Mode addresses pads as notes row*16+column with the right column on notes 8, 24 … 120; Drum Mode uses the drum-rack layout (grid notes 36–99 in four 4×4 blocks, right column notes 100–107).

Chromatic scale: the lanes are C D E F G A B C like the C major scale (they follow the Root). With a track on Chromatic, Pad B top row buttons 5 and 6 become ♭ and ♯ instead of Root −/+. Press ♯, then press a note to sharpen it (or an empty pad to create a sharpened note), or press a Pad B lane button to sharpen every note of that lane. Press ♭ to flatten in the same way. Press the button again to leave the mode. Pressing a sharpened note in flat mode (or the other way round) brings it back to natural. E and B cannot be sharpened, F and C cannot be flattened (ignored). Deleting a note removes its accidental. Sharp notes are drawn lighter and flat notes darker, with ♯ or ♭ on screen.

On-screen edges: every pad on screen has the Launchpad's top row above it, its Novation logo LED, and its right column beside it, with the same functions and LED colours. Click or tap them like the hardware buttons. They also appear around the velocity and gate sliders, so you can switch views from the screen. Pad A's right column picks the track (T1–T4), and Mode, Prob, Gate and Vel open the stepping, gate and velocity modes. Pad B's right column buttons are the lanes (8 at the top, 1 at the bottom).

Both pads: the 8×8 grid toggles notes for the selected track (bottom row = lowest pitch).

Clock input: start/stop/continue messages from this port start and stop the sequencer. With External clock checked, tempo follows its MIDI clock (24 per quarter note) and the BPM field shows the measured tempo.

Top row button 8 (both pads): extend a note. Press it, press an existing note, then press a step in the same lane before or after it. The note plays as one held note across all those steps. Press a covered step later to shorten the note.

Top row button 7 (both pads): swap the pads. Pad A's role (steps 1–8, its controls) moves to the other Launchpad and vice versa.

Pad A top row: play/stop, mute, shorten sequence, lengthen sequence (1–16 steps), octave − / + for the selected track. Mute toggles a mute mode in which you can press a Pad B right-column button (lane), a Pad A right-column track button (track), a bottom-row pad (all notes in that step on the selected track) or the Play button (mute the whole app, sequence keeps running). Press Mute again to leave the mode. Clear and randomize are now on screen only. Pad A right column (top 4): select track 1–4.

Tempo divider on the pads: press Mode, then BPM− or BPM+ (Pad B top row, button 1 or 2; they show Div). The four top rows of both pads become one slider per track (top row = track 1, fourth row = track 4) with 16 positions across the two pads: the leftmost pad is 1 and the rightmost pad is 16. Press a pad to set that track's tempo divider. Press Mode again (or any button) to leave.

Pad B top row: tempo −5 / +5, scale prev / next, root − / + (all tracks). Press Mode first, then Root − / + (they turn yellow) to change the root of the selected track only. When a root wraps from B to C the octave goes up by 1, from C to B down by 1. Oct− / Oct+ on Pad A are toggles: press the lit button again to cancel.

Pad A right column, third from the bottom (Vel): toggles the velocity view. Both pads show vertical sliders, one per step, and pressing a pad in a column sets the velocity of all notes in that step (coarse, 8 levels). Use the on-screen sliders for exact values (1–127, default 100). Press the button again to return to the notes.

Pad A right column, below T4 (Prob): toggles the probability view (dim green). Same layout as the velocity view: a column sets the chance, from 13% to 100%, that the step is played (default 100%). Press the bottom pad of a column twice for 0% (never). On-screen sliders give exact values from 0 to 100. All notes of a step play or are skipped together.

Master slider (slider views): in the velocity, gate and probability views the Pad B right column becomes a slider. Pressing one of its buttons sets ALL steps of the selected track to that level (in the probability view press the bottom button twice for 0%). The lit buttons show the average of the steps. On screen it is one vertical slider beside Pad B.

Pad A right column, below Prob (Gate): toggles the gate view (dim blue). Same layout as the velocity view: a column sets the gate length of the notes in that step, from 13% to 100% of the step (default 50%). Extended notes are not affected.

Pad A right column, bottom button (Mode): choose the stepping mode of the selected track. Press it, then press one of these once: Track 1 forward (default), Track 2 reverse, Track 3 ping-pong, Track 4 random, Prob random walk, Vel Key mode (press Mode again to cancel). Gate switches Jump on or off for the track: while it is on the bottom row glows cyan, and holding a bottom-row pad holds the track's playhead on that step (the step is replayed every tick) until you let go; then the stepping mode carries on from that step. Lanes whose loop range does not contain that step ignore it. Bottom-row pads do not toggle notes while Jump is on. Forward, reverse and ping-pong run every lane within its own start/end range. Random and random walk move a single playhead for the whole track (random walk steps one step up or down), and every lane plays that step if it is inside the lane's range. To set one lane only: press the stepping button, then a lane button (Pad B right column), then a mode button. That lane then follows its own mode (random modes move that lane alone). Choosing a mode for the whole track clears the lane settings.

Key mode (Mode, then Vel): the selected track stops playing its sequence (all its settings are kept; the other tracks keep running) and both pad grids become the same keyboards for the selected track, like the Launchpad Mini's default note layout: each of the four keyboards has two rows (white keys below, black keys above D E G A B, i.e. C♯ D♯ F♯ G♯ A♯ one column to the right of their natural note), covering four octaves from the bottom up. The second keyboard from the bottom is the track's octave and is drawn in the track colour. On a diatonic scale each keyboard is one row of scale notes starting at the root on the left. On the Drums scale each pad shows eight coloured drum pads (4×2 note pads each, lowest bottom left, highest top right), the same on both pads; Pad A drums sound while held, Pad B drums retrigger in step time (tempo and Tempo ÷) while held. Pad A keys sound while held; Pad B keys are latched (press again to stop). Velocity: the Pad B right column is a slider (10–127, default 100). The top row of the grid shows the track's sequencer playhead as a red LED. Play/Stop, Mute (muting tracks; a muted track's keyboard is silent), Oct−/Oct+ (direct), Scale and Root work, and T1–T4 switch tracks: each track keeps its own mode (keyboard or sequencer), so the pads change accordingly; Mode, then Vel leaves Key mode.

Pad B right column: one button per note lane. Press it, then press a pad in that lane for the start step and another for the end step. Press the button again to cancel. Lanes with a custom loop show dark blue between start and end, with brighter blue at the start and end steps.
